Where the data comes from

Signal Atlas will use meteorological observations from the NASA POWER Daily API. The project will document how the data is retrieved, processed, and presented.

About Signal Atlas

Signal Atlas turns meteorological data into clear, accessible explanations of growing conditions across Nigeria.

Signal Atlas is an educational environmental-data project.
